Stocked

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

Stocked is a kitchen inventory, recipe, and grocery management app for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. Stocked is designed to keep your data on your own devices and in your own private iCloud account.

Summary

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking. Your inventory, recipes, and grocery lists are stored on your device and synced through your private iCloud account. Optional features (like receipt scanning and Apple Health logging) only run when you turn them on.

Information Stocked Handles

Account & Sign In

Stocked offers Sign in with Apple or a Guest mode. With Sign in with Apple, Apple provides a private identifier and, on first sign-in only, your name and (optionally) email. Stocked stores this locally to personalize your profile and, if you choose, associate a household. In Guest mode, only the name you enter is stored on-device. Stocked never receives your Apple password.

Kitchen & Recipe Data

Items you add — inventory, quantities, expiration/use-by dates, recipes, grocery and low-stock lists, meal plans, and cooking history — are stored locally on your device and, when enabled, synced through Apple's iCloud/CloudKit under your own Apple ID. This data is not accessible to us as the developer.

Household Sharing

If you create or join a household, the data you choose to share (inventory, shared meal planner, leftovers, and member permissions) is synced so household members can see it. This sync is handled through a secure endpoint operated for Stocked solely to relay household data between members. Only members you invite can access a shared household.

Receipt & Barcode Scanning (Optional)

If you use receipt scanning, an image or its extracted text may be processed to identify purchased grocery items and add them to your inventory. Barcode scanning uses your camera to look up product details. Camera access is requested only when you use these features and is never used in the background.

Apple Health (Optional)

If you opt in, Stocked can log nutrition information to Apple Health when you complete a recipe. This uses Apple's HealthKit with your explicit permission. Stocked does not read your other health data and never shares HealthKit data with third parties or uses it for advertising.

Recipe Sources

When you browse or search external recipe and drink sources, requests are made to those services to retrieve recipes. These requests are limited to what is needed to fetch content and do not include your personal kitchen data.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide app functionality: tracking your kitchen, suggesting and displaying recipes, building grocery lists, syncing across your devices and household, and — where you opt in — logging nutrition to Apple Health. Stocked does not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Data may be transmitted only to: Apple (iCloud/CloudKit and HealthKit, under your account), the Stocked household sync endpoint (only for households you join), and third-party recipe services you choose to browse. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data stays on your device and in your iCloud account until you remove it. Stocked includes in-app controls to Clear All App Data and Delete iCloud Data. Deleting the app and clearing iCloud data removes associated records. Household data you contributed can be removed by leaving or deleting the household.

Security

Stocked relies on Apple's device encryption and iCloud security. Household sync uses encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard practices.

Children's Privacy

Stocked is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
