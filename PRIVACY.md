# Stocked Privacy Policy

**Effective and last updated: August 8, 2026**

Stocked is a kitchen inventory, grocery, recipe, and meal-planning app from Sowens Studios. It contains no advertising SDK and does not use personal information for cross-app tracking or sell personal information.

## Information stored on your device

Stocked stores kitchen inventory, grocery lists, prices, receipts and scan results, recipes, meal plans, cooking history, dietary preferences, household settings, profile information, app preferences, and images you choose to add. The app and its extensions use local files, preferences, and an App Group to keep features and widgets current.

Camera and photo-library access is used only for features you select, including receipt, barcode, list, plate, recipe, profile, and issue-report images. Microphone or speech access is used for voice cooking controls when enabled. Coarse location is used on-device to find nearby grocery stores and is not stored by Sowens Studios. Notifications are used for reminders and alerts you enable.

## Accounts, iCloud, and households

You may use Sign in with Apple or continue as a guest. Stocked stores your Apple subject identifier and profile details needed to remember your account. Apple may provide a private relay email. Session credentials are used to authenticate requests to the Stocked service.

When enabled, private iCloud and CloudKit features store backups and synchronize kitchen or household data through your Apple account. Household members can see data shared into that household, such as inventory, groceries, recipes, plans, activity, and display names. Apple processes iCloud and Sign in with Apple information under its policies.

The Stocked Cloudflare service may also store a household snapshot, member/device identifiers, invitations, changes, tombstones, daily-brief data, rate-limit records, and security logs so household synchronization and requested notifications work across platforms.

## AI, receipt, and recipe processing

Stocked offers on-device Apple Intelligence paths where supported. When a cloud AI feature is used, the information necessary for that request is sent to the Stocked Worker and then to its configured AI provider, currently Anthropic. Depending on the feature, this can include receipt images or OCR text, item names and prices, barcodes, inventory, recipes, dietary preferences, cooking requests, corrections, or issue-report context.

The Worker may temporarily cache selected normalized results, such as barcode or recipe-processing responses, to improve reliability and cost. Cloudflare observability and the AI provider may process request metadata and content under their policies. Do not scan payment-card details or unrelated sensitive documents.

## Other content and data providers

Requested searches may contact providers such as TheMealDB, Spoonacular, USDA FoodData Central, Edamam, API Ninjas, RapidAPI services, retailer endpoints, recipe websites, and Sowens Studios content hosting. Requests can include search terms, barcodes, ingredient names, recipe URLs, and provider credentials included with the app. Each provider applies its own terms and privacy policy.

Approved recipes synchronized from StockedMac may include structured recipe facts, publisher-provided text, the original source URL and attribution, and an image or image URL. Stocked does not claim ownership of third-party recipe or publisher content. The shared service normalizes titles, removes duplicate records, and rejects recipes without a usable image, instructions, or source provenance. Contact support for a source correction or rights-holder removal request.

## Health data

If you explicitly enable the feature, Stocked may write cooked-meal nutrition to Apple Health. Stocked does not use Health information for advertising, tracking, data brokerage, credit, or employment decisions. Health information is handled through HealthKit permissions and remains subject to Apple's Health privacy controls. The current feature is intended to write selected nutrition records and does not require uploading Health data to Sowens Studios.

## Diagnostics and support

Stocked may generate local QA and diagnostic reports. A report or screenshot is sent only when you choose to submit it. Cloudflare may process IP address, timestamps, endpoint, response status, and security/rate-limit information. Support email contains the address, message, and attachments you send.

## Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you remove it or erase the app. Use **Settings → Account → Delete Account** to leave a household, remove supported iCloud backups, and erase local account data. Use Apple settings to manage remaining iCloud information. Household or service records may remain briefly in backups, synchronization tombstones, security logs, caches, or records required to protect other members.

To request deletion of server-held Stocked data or support correspondence, email [support@sowensstudios.com](mailto:support@sowensstudios.com) and include the household/member identifier shown in Settings where available. External providers retain information under their own controls.

## Children, security, and changes

Stocked is a general-audience household utility and is not directed to children under 13. We use HTTPS, signed sessions, Apple platform security, access controls, and rate limits, but no system is completely secure. We may update this policy as features change and will revise the date above.

Privacy questions: [support@sowensstudios.com](mailto:support@sowensstudios.com).
