## Description

Your kitchen, always ready.

Stocked brings inventory, groceries, recipes, meal planning, and cooking tools into one thoughtful home for your kitchen. Know what is available, spot what is running low, and move from a receipt to a realistic meal plan without juggling lists.

BUILD A LIVING INVENTORY
Organize food by storage zone, quantity, container, expiration, nutrition, store, and price. Add items manually, scan a barcode, photograph a list, or review items found on a receipt.

SHOP WITH A SMARTER LIST
Keep groceries grouped and ready, reconcile purchases, compare recorded prices, and share changes with your household.

COOK WHAT YOU HAVE
Browse saved recipes, see what is ready now, identify missing ingredients, scale servings, use substitutions, and follow a focused cooking flow with timers and voice controls.

PLAN THE WEEK
Build meal plans around your inventory, preferences, equipment, schedule, and leftovers. Cooking history and practical insights help reduce waste over time.

SCAN AND IMPORT
Turn receipts, recipe text, supported web pages, and photos into reviewable kitchen information. On-device intelligence is used where available; optional cloud intelligence handles supported requests when needed.

SYNC YOUR HOUSEHOLD
Sign in with Apple to back up supported data and coordinate inventory, groceries, recipes, and plans with people you invite. Guest mode keeps the experience on one device.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Stocked contains no advertising SDK and does not use your information for cross-app tracking. You control camera, photos, location, microphone, Health, notifications, iCloud, and account deletion from system or app settings.

Some searches and intelligent features use third-party services and require a network connection. Suggestions and nutrition estimates are informational, may be incomplete, and are not medical or dietary advice. Always follow food-safety guidance and verify allergies and dietary restrictions yourself.
