STOCKED COOK LATER COMMAND CENTER - TEST PLAN

1. Build all three targets in Xcode 16 with Swift 6 language mode and default main-actor isolation. Test the app, widgets, and share extension.

2. Open the Cook tab. Verify Cook Now still communicates immediate cooking and Cook Later displays “Plan it. Shop for it. Prep it. Cook it.” at every supported size and layout.

3. Open Cook Later and switch repeatedly among Plan, Shop, and Prep. Verify one navigation stack and one planned-meal data source are used; there should be no duplicate planner state.

4. In Plan, verify the readiness ring and counts update after adding, editing, moving, completing, and deleting meals.

5. Test every add-meal source: My Recipes, Based on My Inventory, Use Something Up, Online Suggestions, Recent Meals, and Add My Own Meal. Confirm sheets dismiss cleanly and saved meals appear on the selected day and slot.

6. Open a planned meal. Verify serving changes recalculate ingredient requirements. Check On Hand, Running Low, and Needed states and add one or all shortages to Grocery.

7. Create two meals that require more of the same ingredient than inventory contains. Verify the second/allocation-sensitive checks show a conflict or running-low state and identify competing meals.

8. Open Shop. Verify duplicate shortages combine, quantities remain editable, each row lists its planned-meal provenance, and Add All performs one clean grocery update without duplicate rows.

9. Plan a meal needing heavy cream. Open substitutions. When Half & Half is in inventory, verify it is marked as already available. Apply the substitute and confirm every affected planned meal is updated.

10. Open Prep. Verify freezer proteins generate thaw tasks and suitable ingredients generate marinate, chop, or batch-prep tasks. Complete a task, leave Cook Later, reopen it, and confirm completion persists.

11. Open the calendar overview. Verify dots/legend for planned, cooked, prep, and shopping states. Select a date inside the current seven-day planning horizon and confirm the workspace focuses that day. Dates outside the persisted horizon should not create phantom meals.

12. From Inventory, use Plan with Ingredient and verify the context card shows available, planned, and unallocated quantity. From Recipes, verify Plan This Meal routes to Cook Later. From Grocery, verify planned-meal context lands on Shop.

13. Verify contextual inventory filters and cached online suggestions do not issue network requests on each keystroke and horizontal rails snap without leaving blank trailing space.

14. With a household account on two devices, add/edit/move planned meals and grocery needs. Verify background sync refreshes Plan and Shop without requiring a relaunch and does not duplicate operations.

15. Verify widget timelines refresh after relevant plan/grocery changes and unrelated cache deletion does not delete meals, groceries, or saved recipes.

16. Run the Stocked test target, including CookLaterPlanningTests, AIResponseDecoderTests, HouseholdMergePolicyTests, FoodIntelligenceTests, ReceiptProcessingServiceTests, and BuildConfigurationGuardTests.

17. Test light/dark appearance, Dynamic Type, iPhone SE-sized layouts, standard iPhone, and iPad. Check that Plan/Shop/Prep controls, meal details, quantity steppers, calendar, and sheet detents remain usable.
