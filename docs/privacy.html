Privacy Policy

Stocked has no advertising SDK and does not use personal information for cross-app tracking or sell it.

Kitchen information

Stocked stores inventory, groceries, receipts and scan results, recipes, plans, cooking history, dietary preferences, profile and household information, app settings, and images you add. Camera, photos, microphone/speech, coarse location, notifications, and Health are used only for features and permissions you select.

Accounts and household sync

Sign in with Apple may provide an account identifier and relay email. Guest mode is available. Optional iCloud/CloudKit and the Stocked Cloudflare service synchronize supported kitchen and household information across invited devices. The service may hold household snapshots, member/device identifiers, changes, daily briefs, rate-limit records, and security logs.

AI and providers

Supported on-device intelligence remains local. Cloud features can send receipt images or text, barcodes, inventory, recipes, dietary preferences, cooking requests, corrections, or diagnostics to the Stocked Worker and Anthropic. Searches may contact recipe, nutrition, retailer, and food-data providers. Their policies apply. Do not scan payment details or unrelated sensitive documents.

Health and location

Coarse location is used on-device for nearby stores. If enabled, Stocked writes selected meal nutrition to Apple Health. Health data is not used for advertising, tracking, brokerage, credit, or employment and is not required to be uploaded to Sowens Studios.

Deletion

Use Settings → Account → Delete Account to leave a household, remove supported iCloud backups, and erase local data. For server-held information, email support@sowensstudios.com with the household/member identifier. Limited backups, tombstones, caches, and security records may persist temporarily.
